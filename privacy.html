Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026 • Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Summary for Players: SMISH Ventures values your privacy. We design our games so your core game progress and simulation saves remain on your device. We do not sell your personal data. This policy clearly outlines what minimal technical data is collected, how analytics and ads operate, and your legal rights under GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

1. Introduction & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, websites, and associated services operated by SMISH Ventures ("we", "us", "our"), including but not limited to Movie Mogul: Studio Tycoon, Founder Sim, Mafia Mogul: Crime Tycoon, and Vault Crackers: Puzzle Games (collectively, the "Services").

For the purpose of applicable data protection laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Data Controller is:

SMISH Ventures
Email: hey@smishventures.com
Website: https://smishventures.com

2. Information We Collect and Process

We collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to deliver, maintain, troubleshoot, and optimize gameplay.

A. Game Save Data & Preferences (Stored Locally)

Your active game saves (for example studio progress, box office history, talent contracts, levels cleared, stars earned, in-game currency, purchased items, and settings) are stored locally on your device via secure client storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage / on-device app preferences). SMISH Ventures does not store or read your save files on private external servers.

B. Technical & Diagnostic Information

When you launch or interact with our mobile games, standard non-identifying telemetry may be processed automatically:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version (iOS / iPadOS), screen resolution, preferred language, and system locale.
  • Performance & Crash Logs: Stack traces, frame rate drops, crash timestamps, and diagnostic memory stats to help our engineers isolate and resolve bugs.

C. In-App Purchases & Financial Data

All in-app purchases (e.g., Starter Packs, Unlimited Era Passes, and Ad-Free upgrades) are processed directly by Apple Inc. through the official Apple App Store (StoreKit) or, on Android, by Google Play Billing. SMISH Ventures never receives, processes, or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address. We only receive an anonymous transaction receipt verifying whether an entitlement is active.

D. Advertising & Analytics Identifiers

Depending on the game and your region, we may integrate third-party SDKs to measure anonymous gameplay statistics and display optional rewarded advertisements:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): Gameplay and purchase events (e.g., tutorial completion rates, session duration, levels started and finished, studio stage reached, offers viewed, purchases attempted or completed), together with an app instance identifier, device type, OS version, and approximate country. This data is not linked to your name, email, or any account.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob / Apple Search Ads): In games featuring advertising, ad networks may request your device's advertising identifier (the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, or the Advertising ID on Android). In strict adherence to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, we will only request or use tracking identifiers if you explicitly grant permission when prompted. If you decline or enable "Ask App not to Track", you will only receive contextual, non-personalized ads.
  • Ad Consent (Google User Messaging Platform): Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), a consent form is shown before any personalized advertising, and ads respect the choice you make there.

E. Notifications

Some games (including Vault Crackers) can send optional reminders, such as a daily reward being ready. These are local notifications scheduled on your device; they are only sent if you allow notifications when your device asks, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings. We do not run a push-notification server or collect a push token for them.

F. Vault Crackers: Puzzle Games, at a Glance

Vault Crackers is a single-player, offline puzzle game for iOS, iPadOS, and Android. Specifically:

  • No account or login: We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any sign-up. There is no chat, social feature, or user-generated content.
  • Progress stays on your device: Levels, stars, in-game Cash and Gems, crew, outfits, and settings are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • Purchases: Processed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat verifies store receipts and delivers your purchases using an anonymous app user ID; it receives your purchase history but no payment details.
  • Ads: Optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads are served by Google AdMob. Personalized ads and tracking are used only after you give permission (ATT on iOS; Google's consent form where required).
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay and purchase events (for example level number, result, stars, time taken, and which store item was bought) to help us balance difficulty and improve the game. Because there are no accounts, this data is not linked to your identity, and it can be reset by reinstalling the app.
  • Tracking: Data may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites only for advertising, and only after you grant permission through the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process the information described above strictly for legitimate purposes:

  • To deliver gameplay features, manage game state, and restore purchased entitlements;
  • To identify, debug, and fix software defects, crashes, and performance bottlenecks;
  • To analyze aggregated, non-personally identifiable trends to balance game difficulty and economic mechanics;
  • To respond promptly to player inquiries and technical support tickets sent to our support desk;
  • To comply with statutory legal obligations and protect our rights against fraudulent transactions.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / UK Data Protection)

For players located within the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, our lawful bases under GDPR Article 6 are:

  • Performance of a Contract: Providing the game, processing transactions, and saving progress.
  • Legitimate Interests: Debugging software, maintaining network security, and preventing fraud.
  • Consent: Serving personalized ads or tracking identifiers under the Apple ATT framework, which you may revoke at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party technology providers who adhere to strict data processing agreements:

  • Apple Inc. (App Store & iCloud): For payment processing, app distribution, and optional user-managed cloud backups. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC (Firebase, Google Analytics, AdMob, User Messaging Platform & Google Play): For analytics, crash telemetry, advertising delivery, ad consent, and Android app distribution and payments. Subject to Google's Privacy & Terms; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: For verifying App Store and Google Play receipts and delivering in-app purchases, using an anonymous app user ID. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not rent, sell, or trade your personal data to data brokers or unrelated third parties.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & Age Appropriateness)

Our games are designed for a general audience, including players who enjoy business simulation, tycoon strategy, and casual puzzles. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions). If we discover that personal data of a minor has been collected without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to permanently delete such information. Parents or guardians may contact us at hey@smishventures.com.

7. Data Retention & Security

Local game save data remains on your physical hardware until you choose to delete the application or clear its local storage. Analytics and advertising data held by Google, and purchase records held by RevenueCat, are retained according to those providers' own retention settings and policies. Any diagnostic server logs or support email communications are retained only as long as necessary to address player inquiries and maintain security, after which they are securely purged.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Global Rights)

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full ownership over your privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of what data, if any, is associated with your support interactions.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the deletion of all support records and associated communications. (Local game data can be deleted instantly by uninstalling the application from your device).
  • Right to Opt-Out of Tracking: You can enable or disable personalized ad tracking at any time by navigating on your device to: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > [Game Name]. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID under Settings > Google > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Right to Turn Off Notifications: You can disable game reminders at any time in your device's notification settings.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never penalize or alter gameplay features because you exercised your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at hey@smishventures.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request".

When changes are published, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of our games after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
