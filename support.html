Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open the in-game Store or Settings menu in Movie Mogul or Founder Sim and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed into the same Apple ID used to make the purchase, all permanent purchases will be restored to your device immediately with zero additional charge.

Are SMISH Ventures games playable offline?

Yes. Both Movie Mogul and Founder Sim are designed to run completely offline without an active internet connection. You can play anywhere, including on flights and offline commutes. An internet connection is only needed when making App Store purchases.

Can I sync my save between my iPhone and iPad?

Currently, game progress and career saves are stored locally on your device and do not automatically sync across separate devices. However, any non-consumable in-app purchases you have made can be restored on any device using the "Restore Purchases" option in the game.

How do I report a glitch or balance issue?

We actively listen to player feedback and release regular patches. Please email us at hey@smishventures.com with the game name, device model, iOS version, and a brief description (or screenshot) of what occurred.

How do I request a refund for an in-app purchase?

Because all App Store financial transactions are processed directly by Apple Inc., developers do not have direct access to your payment methods. To request a refund, visit Apple's official portal at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select "Request a refund".
