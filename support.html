Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone or iPad?

In either Movie Mogul or Founder Sim, tap the Settings gear icon in the main menu > tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed into the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase, your entitlements (such as Unlimited Era Pass, expansion packages, or Ad-Free upgrades) will be restored instantly with zero additional charge.

Are SMISH Ventures games playable offline?

Yes! Both Movie Mogul and Founder Sim are engineered to run completely offline without an active internet connection. You can produce blockbuster films, direct stars, and negotiate studio deals while on airplanes, subways, or remote locations. Online connectivity is only required if you choose to make in-app purchases or watch optional rewarded ads.

Can I sync my save between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Ensure that iCloud Drive is enabled in your device's Apple ID settings for the game. Your career saves and studio milestones synchronize automatically across all your registered iOS and iPadOS hardware.

How do I report a glitch or balance issue?

We actively monitor feedback and issue balance patches. Please email us at support@smishventures.com with your device model, iOS version, studio stage, and a screenshot if possible.

How do I request a refund for an in-app purchase?

Because all App Store financial transactions are processed directly by Apple Inc., developers do not have direct access to your payment methods. To request a refund, visit Apple's official portal at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select "Request a refund".
